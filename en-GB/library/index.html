The Official Great Library of the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company

Please visit our recommendations page →

The Official Great Library of the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is our company's most treasured creation. We strive to, in accordance with our strict quality and accuracy policies, create the most comprehensive and complete archival library on Communism and Socialism in the world. One of our fundamental values that incited the creation of our library, is the Open Access Initiative.

In accordance with this initiative and our meticulous endeavours on providing high-standard theoretical works and educational materials, our research institute has curated a list for our visitors that includes the foundational works on Communism and Socialism and we hereby recommend this list as a must-read for all scholars and researchers alike.

We are honoured to engage in the joint publishing and exchange of publications with our esteemed partners at the

Foreign Languages Publishing House of the DPR Korea, The Marxists Internet Archive and BannedThought.net.

These joint publishing endeavours are in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, the BeNeLux Union and the European Union—in which we are incorporated.

We are proud to include our own theoretical works, educational materials and other publications in our library. These works, materials and publications are written and curated by our research institute: the Köhn-van den Boomgaard Institute for the Theoretical Study of Scientific Socialism and Communism.

The Köhn-van den Boomgaard Institute for the Theoretical Study of Scientific Socialism and Communism accompanies us in our scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of our great library's selection by collecting and reviewing primary-source works, materials and publications originating from the current and former Communist and Socialist countries of the world.

Our research institute's personnel includes scholars of China and East Asia who are committed to providing scholars and researchers alike with access to original documents in their historical context.

For more information about our research institute and our library's inclusions, please visit our about page →
