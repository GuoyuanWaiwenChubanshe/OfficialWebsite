Kim Il Sung

(1912-1994)

Biographical Summary

With the Turning of the Century

Entering the modern times, Korea, with its 5,000-year-long history and brilliant culture, gradually weakened in national power, for the feudal Joson dynasty's reactionary policy of flunkeyism and slavish dependence on outside forces did little to halt its decline. Exposed to great-power competition, the country was reduced to a theatre for the imperialist powers, and, alas, placed under Japanese military occupation in 1905. But there was hope, for it was none other than Comrade Kim Il Sung who would save the Korean nation from the miserable fate of ruination.

Birth and lineage

Born in Mangyongdae, Pyongyang, on April 15, Juche 1 (1912), to a revolutionary family devoted to Korea's liberation and the people's happiness, Kim Il Sung embarked upon the road of revolutionary struggle for Korea’s liberation in his teens. His father, Kim Hyong Jik, was a prominent leader of the national liberation movement, and his mother, Kang Pan Sok, was a leader of the communist women's movement.

Childhood and early education

Kim Il Sung spent his childhood under the harsh conditions of Japanese colonial rule. Kim Il Sung received his early education in Korea and China, studying Marxism-Leninism and taking part in revolutionary activities. In the course of groping for the road of the Korean revolution, Kim Il Sung formed the Down-with-Imperialism Union on October 17, Juche 15 (1926), beginning the organised revolutionary struggle against Japanese imperialism.

Revolutionary struggle

Kim Il Sung devoted himself to authoring the Juche idea and the Songun idea whilst simultaneously commanding the anti-Japanese revolutionary struggle for two decades. Kim Il Sung founded the Korean People's Revolutionary Army and led the anti-Japanese revolutionary struggle until finally achieving the historic cause of Korea’s liberation on August 15 Juche 34 (1945).

Liberation and founding of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea

Following his triumphal return to the liberated homeland, Kim Il Sung lost no time in founding the Workers' Party of Korea. Kim Il Sung carried out the democratic reforms, such as the land reforms, indstrial nationalisation and the Law on Gender Equality, whilst simultaneously forging a regular armed force. Based on these historic accomplishments, Kim Il Sung founded the Democratic People's Republic of Korea on September 9, Juche 37 (1948), thus establishing the first democratic state in the East.

The Korean War

Kim Il Sung heroically safeguarded the sovereignty and dignity of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea in the Korean War(1950-1953), provoked by the US in an attempt to stifle the two-year-old Republic in its cradle. Kim Il Sung carried out the socialist revolution after the war, leading the socialist construction of several stages to victory, thus turning the Democratic People's Republic of Korea into a socialist power, independent in politics, self-sufficient in the economy and self-reliant in defence.

The Peaceful Reunification of Korea

Kim Il Sung put forward the principles and proposals for achieving the independent reunification of the country without interference from outside powers, including the Three Principles of National Reunification, the Ten-Point Programme of the Great Unity of the Whole Nation and the Proposal on Founding the Democratic Federal Republic of Koryo, and devoted his all for the national reunification cause until the last day of his life.

International support

Kim Il Sung defined independence, peace and friendship as the basic ideal of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea's foreign policy and enhanced its international prestige through his energetic external activities. Kim Il Sung supported national liberation struggles and anti-imperialist movements all over the world and strengthened international solidarity. Kim Il Sung made contributions to the development of relations between Korea and other countries on the basis of independence and mutual respect.

Global Praise

Amongst the praise of the Juche Korean people and the socialists and progressives all over the world, former US President Jimmy Carter said that President Kim Il Sung was greater than the three American Presidents who had represented the nation-building and destiny of the United States-George Washington, Thomas Jefferson and Abraham Lincoln-put together.

Passing and immortalisation

Kim Il Sung continued to lead the Korean revolution and socialist construction tirelessly until his passing on July 8, Juche 83 (1994). Showing his devotion to Korea and the Juche Korean people. The Democratic People's Republic of Korea posthumously immortalised Kim Il Sung with the title of Eternal President on the occasion of the 1st Session of the 10th Supreme People's Assembly on September 5, Juche 87 (1998). Kim Il Sung will always be alive in the hearts of the Juche Korean people and the world socialists and progressives as the founding father of Juche Korea, pioneer of the cause of independence of humankind, Eternal President of the Republic and the Eternal Leader of Juche Korea.

This page presents official biographical and historical materials from the Democratic People's Republic of Korea as a curated and truthful source for scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations with an interest in historic and contemporary China, Korea, Japan and East Asian studies.
