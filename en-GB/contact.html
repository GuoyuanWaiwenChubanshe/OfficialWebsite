Contact Us

We sincerely welcome scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world to inquire us concerning general inquiries, questions and recommendations, formal inquiries about collaborations and partnerships, our daily tasks, operations and functioning, or the implications of our funding. We are open to engage in partnerships, in accordance with our primary mission, regarding collective learning and intellectual exchange and collaborating with schools, universities and other educational organisations and facilities.

All of the messages received by our office are of great importance to us and will be read thoroughly and adequately replied to within one to two business days.

Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company

The Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is an international nonprofit organisation specialised in the scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of Communist and Socialist theoretical works and educational materials with significant intellectual and literary value. In accordance with our high standards on quality and historical accuracy, we strive to provide scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials.

Our primary mission is to guide, engage and ensure the scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of Communist and Socialist theoretical works and educational materials with significant intellectual and literary value. To provide scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials—and for those works and materials, in their original editions and historical context, to be available to everyone for collective learning and intellectual exchange, without being restricted by cost, location or access barriers.
