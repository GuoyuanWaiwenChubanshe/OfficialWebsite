Recommendations

The book recommendation section brings together a number of literary and theoretical works of significant intellectual and political importance.

We are honored to have included relevant works in our library from the Korean Foreign Languages Publishing House（외국문출판사）and the Marxists Internet Archive（marxists.org）.

This is an important part of the open-sourcing initiative that the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company has been striving towards since the beginning.
