Welcome to the Official Website of the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company; Carefully making books, Reading together in unity

The Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is an international Maoist-aligned Non-profit organisation specialised in the scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of Communist and Socialist theoretical works and educational materials with significant intellectual and literary value. In accordance with our high standards on quality and historical accuracy, we strive to provide scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials.

For more information about us, please visit our about page →

Recommendations

The book recommendation section brings together a number of literary and theoretical works of significant intellectual and political importance.

We are honored to have included relevant works in our library from the Korean Foreign Languages Publishing House（외국문출판사）and the Marxists Internet Archive（marxists.org）.

This is an important part of the open-sourcing initiative that Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company has been striving towards since the beginning.
