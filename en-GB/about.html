Frontpage / About

The Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is an international nonprofit organisation specialised in the scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of Communist and Socialist theoretical works and educational materials with significant intellectual and literary value. In accordance with our high standards on quality and historical accuracy, we strive to provide scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials.

The 果园外文出版社 (Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company) was founded in 2026 by Hana van den Boomgaard, our director. Our greater purpose is to be an international nonprofit organisation facilitating collective learning and intellectual exchange about Communism and Socialism. We strive to show everyone the reality about Communism/Socialism and the former/current Communist/Socialist countries and their theoretical works and educational materials. Providing open access to knowledge and information rarely shown in their actual and truthful context—without being restricted by cost, location or access barriers

Nowadays, many theoretical works and educational materials on Communism and Socialism are widely available within the People's Republic of China , and in mostly reliable editions. In the west, however, they often lack that reliability. Works and materials are often either unavailable altogether, due to political censorship. Or, when they are freely accessible, the translation quality and accuracy varies greatly. This makes it increasingly difficult for readers to determine which editions are authentic and trustworthy.

We make a continuous effort to address this issue by opening our library for independent writers/publishers and publishing/research organisations to, in accordance with our high standards on quality and historical accuracy, engage in a partnership with the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company for the joint publishing and exchange of publications.

In addition to our own works and materials written by our research institute, engaging in these partnerships with other writers and publishers is of great value to strengthen our endeavours on providing all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials.

Curated foundational works and recommendations

The Official Great Library of the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is our company's most treasured creation. We strive to, in accordance with our strict quality and accuracy policies, create the most comprehensive and complete archival library on Communism and Socialism in the world. One of our fundamental values that incited the creation of our library, is the Open Access Initiative.

In accordance with this initiative and our meticulous endeavours on providing high-standard theoretical works and educational materials, our research institute has curated a list for our visitors that includes the foundational works on Communism and Socialism and we hereby recommend this list as a must-read for all scholars and researchers alike.

We are honoured to engage in the joint publishing and exchange of publications with our esteemed partners at the Foreign Languages Publishing House of the DPR Korea, The Marxist Internet Archive and BannedThought.net.

Our reasoning for engaging in these partnerships is, in addition to strengthening our endeavours on making Communist/Socialist works and materials accessible around the world, the similarities in the core principles between our esteemed partners and the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company. All our esteemed partners are advocates of the Open Access Initiative and have extensive experience in the research, translation and dissemination of Communist/Socialist works and materials with significant intellectual and literary value.

The Open Access Initiative aims to make all works, materials and publications in our library freely available to everyone for collective learning and intellectual exchange, without being restricted by cost, location or access barriers, while respecting the rights of authors and publishers.

Detailed information about our tasks, operations and functioning

Please visit our publications page →

The Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is not a conventional publishing company. in addition to being completely non-profit and open source, thereby solely relying on the generous support of volunteers, collaborating organisations and governments/private equity to ensure our functioning, we operate as more of an international public publication platform.

We make a continuous effort to carry out the following tasks:

  • Operating our public publication platform:In accordance with our high standards on quality and historical accuracy, we strive to operate our public publication platform as a central means for providing scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials. This platform is fundamental to our endeavours on making Communist and Socialist theoretical works and educational materials with significant intellectual and literary value accessible around the world, without being restricted by cost, location or access barriers.
  • Engaging in research, translation and dissemination: Our research institute accompanies us in the scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of our library's selection by collecting and reviewing primary-source works, materials and publications originating from the current and former Communist and Socialist countries of the world. In addition to our own works and materials written by our research institute, engaging in partnerships with other writers and publishers is of great value to strengthen our endeavours on providing all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials.
  • Ensuring the authenticity and trustworthiness of our works and materials: In accordance with our strict quality and accuracy policies, we strive to ensure the authenticity and trustworthiness of our works and materials by collecting and reviewing primary-source works, materials and publications. Our research institute's personnel includes scholars of China and East Asia who are committed to providing scholars and researchers alike with access to original documents in their historical context. This makes it possible for readers to determine which editions are authentic and trustworthy.
  • Maintaining and expanding our library: The Official Great Library of the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company is our company's most treasured creation. We strive to, in accordance with our strict quality and accuracy policies, create the most comprehensive and complete archival library on Communism and Socialism in the world. We are proud to include our own theoretical works, educational materials and other publications in our library. We also engage in the joint publishing and exchange of publications with our esteemed partners to expand our library.
  • Supporting collective learning and intellectual exchange: In accordance with our Open Access Initiative and our meticulous endeavours on providing high-standard theoretical works and educational materials, we aim to liberate literature and knowledge from closed source systems, ensuring that all works, materials and publications are available to everyone for collective learning and intellectual exchange, without being restricted by cost, location or access barriers. Our research institute has curated a list for our visitors that includes the foundational works on Communism and Socialism and we hereby recommend this list as a must-read for all scholars and researchers alike.
  • Bridging the gap in education: Nowadays, many theoretical works and educational materials on Communism and Socialism are widely available within the People's Republic of China, and in mostly reliable editions. In the west, however, they often lack that reliability. Works and materials are often either unavailable altogether, due to political censorship. Or, when they are freely accessible, the translation quality and accuracy varies greatly. This makes it increasingly difficult for readers to determine which editions are authentic and trustworthy. We make a continuous effort to address this issue by opening our library for independent writers/publishers and publishing/research organisations to engage in a partnership with the Boomgaard Foreign Languages Publishing Company for the joint publishing and exchange of publications, thereby bridging the gap in education.

Our primary mission

Ultimately, all of these tasks conclude into our primary mission: to guide, engage and ensure the scientific research, accurate translation and conscientious dissemination of Communist and Socialist theoretical works and educational materials with significant intellectual and literary value. To provide scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations from all countries of the world with high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials—and for those works and materials, in their original editions and historical context, to be available to everyone for collective learning and intellectual exchange, without being restricted by cost, location or access barriers.

"果园", "Boomgaard" (orchard) is not an arbitrarily chosen name, it has it's origin as a symbol deeply engraved into chinese culture in addition to it also being the family name of Hana van den Boomgaard, our founder and director. We believe that truly authentic and valuable theoretical works and educational materials take time to develop. The concept of the 果园 (orchard) itself is a metaphor for time and accumulation. The mighty trees in nature do not bear it's fruits overnight. Only with persistent care and unrelenting patience, their roots settle and eventually grow into something that can sustain itself for the healthy and prosperous future years.

In accordance with our primary mission and our believes, this is specifically what we hope to carry out. It is not about seeking short-term popularity, but rather about gradually introducing high-quality, accurate, authentic and trustworthy works and materials that are worth preserving, so that they can continue to have an impact in the future. Those works and materials should not be lost due to language barriers, geographical differences or the passing of time. As long as there are scholars, writers, researchers and publishing/research organisations who continue to research, translate and disseminate them, they will continue to prosper and blossom within the hearts and minds of new future readers.

As for the future, we will continue to expand the scope of our collaborations and partnerships, seeking out and engaging with our partners with ever-growing enthusiasm, while keeping our mission close at heart. We also wholeheartedly invite our readers to engage in recommending any works and materials that they deem to possess significant intellectual and literary value or to share works and materials that have not yet been widely introduced in our library. Our public publication platform is open by nature, in accordance with the Open Access Initiative, and it's cultivation does not belong to just one person. The 果园 (orchard) will blossom over time, just like a real orchard would, continuing to be healthy and prosperous.
